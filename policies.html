A. Privacy Policy

Your personal data and family memories are sacred to us.

Strict Photo Privacy

Zero Selling Guarantee

CrazeeSpot never sells, rents, leases, or trades customer personal information or photos to third-party marketing agencies or advertisers under any circumstances.

1

Customer Information Collection

To craft, customize, and deliver bespoke gifts to your doorstep, CrazeeSpot collects only essential customer details provided voluntarily when placing an order or making an inquiry:

  • Contact Details: Full Name, WhatsApp phone number, and Email address for order status, artwork approvals, and dispatch notifications.
  • Shipping & Delivery Details: Complete recipient address, nearest landmark, town/city, state, and 6-digit postal PIN code for accurate courier routing.
  • Customization Assets: Personal photographs, couple/family portraits, custom text, names, dates, quotes, corporate logos, or specific audio/video clips (for QR soundwave or video frames).
  • Device & Browsing Data: Non-identifiable technical data such as IP address, browser type, and cookies used solely to maintain shopping cart items and improve browsing speed.
2

How Customer Data Is Used

Your information is utilized strictly to fulfill your gifting experience:

  • Handcrafting & Personalization: Designing caricatures, setting photo mosaics, precision laser engraving names, and formatting print layouts.
  • Digital Artwork Approval: Sharing high-resolution previews and artwork mockups on WhatsApp for your feedback and confirmation prior to final manufacturing.
  • Shipping & Logistics: Generating automated courier airway bills (AWB), coordinating delivery with courier partners, and sending live tracking links.
  • Customer Support: Assisting you with questions, special date deadlines, and order inquiries.
3

Data Protection & Photo Confidentiality

We respect the sensitive and intimate nature of personal photographs:

  • Encrypted Access: All customer files and photos are stored on secure, access-restricted internal design workstations accessible only by authorized production artists.
  • Strict Photo Purging: Once an order is delivered and the 7-day replacement window has lapsed, customer photographs are automatically purged or archived securely.
  • No Public Showcasing Without Consent: We never post customer photos, couple portraits, or newborn pictures on our social media channels (Instagram, Facebook) or website showcase without explicit written consent from the customer.
If you wish for your photos or design files to be erased immediately upon parcel delivery, simply notify us on WhatsApp (+91 73975 54214), and our team will erase all source media instantly.
4

Third-Party Payment Processing

All financial transactions are conducted through certified, RBI-compliant banking networks and payment providers:

  • Direct UPI & Gateways: We accept payments via official UPI channels (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking, and Bank Transfers.
  • Zero Financial Storage: CrazeeSpot does NOT collect, view, or store debit/credit card numbers, CVV codes, UPI PINs, or banking passwords.
  • SSL 256-Bit Encryption: All digital interactions and payment gateways utilize military-grade 256-bit SSL encryption to guarantee complete transaction security.

B. Terms & Conditions

Guidelines governing orders, bespoke craftsmanship, and customer commitments.

Custom Craftsmanship

Custom Made Exclusively for You

Every item at CrazeeSpot is individually manufactured based on your unique specifications. By placing an order, you agree to these operational terms.

1

Ordering Process

Placing an order with CrazeeSpot is smooth and personalized:

  • Order Placement: Orders can be initiated via our website product catalog, cart checkout, or directly through our official WhatsApp business desk (+91 73975 54214).
  • Customization Submission: After submitting your order, you will be prompted to share your desired photos, custom names, quotes, or theme preferences via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Order Confirmation: An order is considered officially confirmed and queued for production once advance payment is verified and required customization materials are received.
2

Payment Terms

Our payment policies reflect the customized nature of our products:

  • 100% Advance Payment: Because personalized gifts cannot be re-purposed or resold to any other recipient, 100% advance payment is required before design and fabrication begin.
  • No Cash On Delivery (COD): Cash on Delivery is strictly unavailable for customized or photo-engraved gifts.
  • Currency & Taxes: All prices displayed across the website and catalog are in Indian National Rupees (INR ₹) and are inclusive of standard GST/taxes.
  • Corporate Bulk Orders: For bulk corporate mementos, trophies, or event gift sets exceeding ₹15,000, a 60% advance deposit with the remaining 40% balance payable prior to dispatch may be approved upon request.
3

Product Customization Guidelines & Proofs

To achieve the finest finish, we adhere to high production standards:

  • Image Resolution: Customers are requested to provide clear, high-resolution original photos (avoid screenshots or heavily compressed social media forwards where possible). CrazeeSpot is not liable for blurriness resulting from poor-quality source images provided by the buyer.
  • Digital Proof Approval: For applicable products (such as caricature stands, photo mosaics, LED light frames, and wooden nameboards), our design artists share a digital mock-up preview on WhatsApp. Once the preview is approved by the customer, production commences and further design modifications cannot be accommodated.
  • Natural Material Variations: Handcrafted products using natural wood, pine MDF, acrylic, or hand-poured resin may exhibit minor natural variations in wood grain, knot patterns, or subtle color depth. These natural nuances are the hallmark of genuine artisan gifts.
4

Customer Responsibilities

When ordering with us, customers agree to:

  • Address Accuracy: Double-check the recipient's complete postal address, door number, street, pincode, and an active mobile number. Additional courier re-dispatch fees caused by wrong pincodes or unreachable recipient phones are the customer's responsibility.
  • Content Legitimacy: Ensure that any photos, brand logos, or quotes submitted do not violate intellectual property, copyright, or contain unlawful or offensive materials.
  • Prompt Preview Feedback: Review and approve digital WhatsApp previews promptly so your custom gift can be dispatched within your desired timeline.

C. Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear rules on order cancellations, customized products, and free transit damage replacements.

100% Free Damage Replacement

Zero-Risk Transit Guarantee

If your personalized gift arrives broken or damaged during shipping, we provide a 100% FREE REPLACEMENT expedited immediately at zero extra cost.

1

Cancellation Eligibility

Due to the customized nature of personalized items, cancellation windows are time-sensitive:

  • Within 2 Hours: You may cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it or before our design team has begun work on your artwork/photo editing, whichever occurs first.
  • Non-Cancellable Once In Production: Once laser engraving, photo printing, acrylic cutting, resin casting, or frame fabrication has started, the order enters active production and cannot be cancelled or retracted.
2

Refund Conditions & Timelines

When a refund is approved under our cancellation or store guidelines:

  • Original Payment Method: Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original source account (UPI, Google Pay, Net Banking, or Debit/Credit card).
  • Timeline: The refund will reflect in your account within 5 to 7 business banking days, in accordance with standard bank clearing cycles.
  • Duplicate Transactions: In the event of an accidental double debit or payment processing error, excess funds are refunded within 24–48 hours upon transaction verification.
3

Damaged Product Replacement (100% Free)

We use robust 4-layer shockproof packing (air bubble wrap, corner foam protectors, and rigid corrugated boxes). In the rare instance of rough courier transit damage, we have your back:

  • Mandatory Unboxing Video: To claim a free damage replacement, a clear unboxing video is mandatory. The video must start before opening the outer sealed courier parcel, showing the shipping label, and continuing without cuts or pauses until the damage is clearly visible.
  • 24-Hour Reporting Window: The unboxing video must be submitted to our WhatsApp (+91 73975 54214) or email (beecrazee05@gmail.com) within 24 hours of delivery.
  • Priority Dispatch: Once the damage is verified, our team fabricates a brand new replacement piece and dispatches it with priority express courier at absolutely zero cost to you.
Why an unboxing video? As a custom handcraft studio, an uncut unboxing video helps us immediately hold logistics partners accountable and expedites your replacement without bureaucratic paperwork.
4

Customized Product Refund Conditions

Important clarity regarding customized gifts:

  • No Returns for Change of Mind: Because personalized gifts carry personal names, personal photos, and intimate messages, returns or cash refunds cannot be provided if the customer changes their mind, orders the wrong size, or dislikes an approved design post-delivery.
  • CrazeeSpot Error Guarantee: If an error is caused solely by our production team (e.g., spelling differs from the text approved by the customer, or an incorrect photo was used), CrazeeSpot will replace the item immediately free of charge or provide a full 100% refund without question.

D. Shipping & Delivery Policy

Pan-India courier network, turnaround times, tracking, and delivery timelines.

Pan-India Delivery

Delivering to Every Corner of India

From bustling metros to scenic rural pin codes, we partner with premier courier networks (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC, and India Post Speed Post) to deliver your gifts safely.

1

Pan-India Delivery Coverage

CrazeeSpot delivers across all 28 states and 8 Union Territories in India:

  • Full Pin-Code Reach: We ship to over 19,000+ pin codes across India, covering residential apartments, independent houses, corporate offices, and university campuses.
  • Trusted Courier Partners: Shipments are managed through BlueDart, Delhivery Express, DTDC, Professional Couriers, and Speed Post for remote/hill regions.
  • Gift Packaging: All orders are packed discreetly with premium padding so price invoices are not displayed when sending directly as a surprise gift to your loved ones!
2

Estimated Delivery Time

Delivery time comprises two parts: Handcrafting Time + Transit Time:

  • Handcrafting & Preparation: 1 to 3 business days (laser cutting, digital caricature art, resin curing, framing, and quality check).
  • Tamil Nadu & South India: 2 to 4 business days transit.
  • Major Metro Cities: 3 to 5 business days transit (Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Chennai, Pune, Ahmedabad).
  • Rest of India: 4 to 7 business days transit.
  • Total Estimated Turnaround: Most orders arrive safely at your doorstep within 4 to 8 business days from the date of artwork confirmation.
Need an urgent delivery for a birthday or anniversary? Contact us on WhatsApp (+91 73975 54214) before ordering. We offer expedited priority processing for special deadlines!
3

Shipping Charges

We keep shipping charges transparent, fair, and economical:

  • Free Shipping Threshold: Orders above ₹999 qualify for 100% FREE Standard Shipping across India!
  • Standard Flat Rate: For orders below ₹999, a nominal flat courier fee of ₹60 to ₹99 applies depending on total package weight and volumetric dimensions.
  • Express Air Courier: Available upon special request for urgent orders; actual air-courier surcharge quoted transparently prior to confirmation.
4

Order Tracking

Stay informed every step of the journey:

  • Automated AWB Notification: As soon as your custom gift is packaged and handed over to our logistics partner, you will receive your official Airway Bill (AWB) number and direct courier tracking link via WhatsApp and/or SMS.
  • Live Tracking: You can monitor your parcel's live movement from our hub through sorting hubs right up to out-for-delivery status.
  • Delivery Inquiries: If you need any assistance tracking your parcel, our WhatsApp desk is ready to check the real-time courier status for you.
5

Delivery Delays & Operational Exceptions

While 95%+ of our parcels reach on or before the estimated date, occasional delays may occur due to factors beyond our direct control:

  • External Factors: Extreme weather conditions, heavy rains/flooding, regional curfews, public strikes, national holidays, or peak Diwali/New Year courier network congestion.
  • Incomplete Address / Unreachable Recipient: Couriers usually attempt delivery up to 3 times. If the recipient phone is switched off or the door is locked, please coordinate with the courier driver using the OTP/contact details sent via SMS.
  • Our Commitment: In any case of an unexpected transit hold-up, our dedicated support team actively escalates with regional courier hub managers to ensure your gift is expedited for prompt delivery.
