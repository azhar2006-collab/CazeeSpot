Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about customizing and ordering your gifts.

How do I send photos for Caricatures and Photo Keychains?
You can enter your custom text right on this page before ordering, then click "Order on WhatsApp" (+91 73975 54214) to share your high-resolution photos! We share a digital artwork preview for your approval before crafting.
What is the price of Caricature Wooden Stands?
All Caricature Wooden Stands are available at an exclusive promotional price of just ₹499 (regular ₹999), which includes free custom laser engraving on the natural pine wood base!
Can I choose a specific model out of the 39 available?
Yes! Every keychain has a code (KC-01 to KC-22) and every caricature has a code (CR-01 to CR-17). You can click "Customize" on any card or select your model from the dropdown inside the popup.
How long does shipping take?
Crafting takes 1–2 business days, and delivery takes 3–5 days across India via reliable courier partners. Express delivery options are also available upon request.
Can I place bulk orders for corporate gifts, weddings or return gifts?
Absolutely! We specialize in bulk return gifts for birthdays, weddings, farewells, college batches, and corporate events with special bulk discounts. Reach out on WhatsApp for a quick quote!
