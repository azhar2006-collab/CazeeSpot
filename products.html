Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about customizing and ordering your keychains.

How do I send my photo or custom name for the keychain?
You can either enter your custom name right on this page before ordering, or click "Order Now" to chat on WhatsApp (+91 73975 54214) and send your photos, song link, or names directly! We share a preview for approval before crafting.
Can I choose a specific model out of the 22 available?
Yes! Every keychain has a specific model code (Model #KC-01 to #KC-22). You can click "Customize Model" on any card or switch models inside the customization popup to choose exactly which style you want.
How long does shipping take?
Customization takes 1–2 business days, and delivery takes 3–5 days across India via reliable courier partners. Express delivery options are also available upon request.
Can I place bulk orders for birthdays, weddings, or corporate events?
Absolutely! We specialize in bulk return gifts for birthdays, weddings, farewells, college batches, and corporate events with special discounted bulk rates. Reach out on WhatsApp for a quick quote!
